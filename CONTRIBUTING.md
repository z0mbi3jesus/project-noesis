# Contributing to Project NOESIS

The current public contribution paths are documentation, research-method feedback,
and experiment proposals. Tester downloads are on hold. The implementation is not
included in this prepared public package.

Participation is for helping NOESIS research and development, within the
[approved scope](docs/PARTICIPATION_SCOPE.md). It does not grant ownership or a
general software-use, redistribution, or commercialization license.

## Useful contributions

- Review whether the documented conclusions follow from the evidence.
- Propose experiments without presenting hypotheses as demonstrated abilities.
- Submit documentation corrections through a pull request.
- When tester builds become available, follow the [protocols](docs/TESTING.md) and
  record version, benchmark, operating system, input order, and actual results.
- Report unsuccessful runs as well as successful runs. Explain the smallest
  reproduction steps, including whether you initialized a fresh session.

Use the issue forms. New benchmarks and cognitive mechanisms require discussion
and project-lead approval; proposed changes are not automatic implementation scope.

## Evidence and privacy

Before sharing a diagnostic export, inspect the ZIP. Remove personal text, local
usernames/paths, credentials, and unrelated data. Explain redactions; never change
an experimental result to make it appear successful. Identify whether evidence is
an application export, a screenshot, a tester observation, or an independent check.

Do not upload private source code, internal design files, unreviewed executables,
or complete development directories. See [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) for reports
requiring private handling.

## Discussion

Discuss observations and mechanisms respectfully. Avoid harassment and unsupported
claims. Participation does not imply employment, project ownership, or permission
to publish private project materials.

See [community conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md), [privacy](PRIVACY.md), and
[research integrity](docs/RESEARCH_POLICY.md) for the policies applying to this space.

## Rights in contributions

Read [LICENSE.md](LICENSE.md) before submitting protected material. No general
reuse license has been chosen. Contributors retain copyright in their contributions;
submission alone does not assign ownership to 6th Level LLC.

Before merging a substantive documentation contribution, the maintainer and
contributor should agree in writing on the permission needed to publish and maintain
it. Do not submit material you lack authority to share. Bug observations and research
ideas are welcome, but do not include confidential information. There is no blanket
copyright assignment or contributor license agreement in this policy.
