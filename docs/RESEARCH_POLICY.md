# Research integrity and review

Effective: October 15, 2026

NOESIS uses an incremental process: design, approved implementation, benchmark,
analysis, validation, and a decision about the next step. Exploratory discussion
is not implementation approval.

## Evidence

Reports should identify the application version, benchmark, session, initial
conditions, exact input order, observed results, and relevant limitations.
Distinguish developer-run automated tests, application-generated assessments,
tester observations, and independent checks. Do not claim independent validation
merely because a development test passed.

Keep successful and unsuccessful results inspectable. Record expected rejections
separately from unexpected processing failures. Correct published mistakes openly;
do not silently change evidence to match a desired conclusion. Identify redactions,
missing records, and changes to methods or expected results.

## Claims and measurement

Programmed symbol handling is not evidence by itself of semantic understanding,
generalized learning, autonomous reasoning, consciousness, or independent cognition.
Architectural names and aspirations are not demonstrated capabilities.

Define checks before evaluating a result. Preserve complete prior-encounter
references where a benchmark requires them. Separate functional correctness,
session lifecycle, formal benchmark assessment, and researcher acceptance.
Stopping a session does not establish a passing benchmark.

Performance reports must describe measurement scope, units, hardware/software
context where available, repetitions, instrumentation overhead, and excluded costs.
Single-run measurements are observations, not statistical trends or speed guarantees.

## Participation and publication

Credit relevant existing work and contributors accurately. Obtain permission before
sharing materials belonging to others, and disclose what remains unavailable for
independent review. Public benchmark specifications do not imply that private source
or internal datasets are available. Withholding implementation limits reproducibility
and should be stated explicitly.

The project lead approves new benchmark scope and changes to research direction.
Community proposals are welcome, but do not create a promise of implementation,
funding, publication credit, or a release date.
