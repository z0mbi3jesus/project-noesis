# Research-and-development participation

Effective: October 15, 2026

NOESIS currently invites participation for the purpose of helping its research and
development. It is a community-involved project with restricted permissions, not
an open-source implementation release.

## Current participation

- Review and discuss the public research materials and benchmark specifications.
- Submit research questions, experiment proposals, documentation corrections,
  bug observations, and evidence-based criticism through the project channels.
- Perform approved testing when an application is separately made available under
  its applicable terms. Tester downloads remain on hold.

This invitation does not grant access to unpublished materials, a general software
use or modification license, or permission to redistribute software, commercialize
protected project materials, or use NOESIS branding beyond existing lawful rights.
Those uses require separate written authorization from the relevant rights holder.
See [LICENSE.md](../LICENSE.md) and [branding guidance](BRANDING.md).

Proposed documentation edits may be submitted for review through GitHub's normal
contribution mechanisms. Publication or further reuse of a substantive contribution
requires the contributor permissions described in [CONTRIBUTING.md](../CONTRIBUTING.md).
No general documentation reuse license is selected by this participation policy.
Applicable law, GitHub's terms, and separately granted permissions remain unaffected.

## Ownership and contributions

6th Level LLC retains ownership of project materials in which it holds rights.
Participation does not transfer project ownership or grant a share in the project.
Contributors retain their respective rights unless a separate agreement says
otherwise. This policy does not create an employment arrangement, copyright
assignment, or blanket contributor license agreement.

## Possible future open-source release

An open-source release may be considered after the CAC foundation is more clearly
defined and experimentally validated. It is not promised or automatic. The project
lead must explicitly approve the released components and license after confirming
the authority to license all included contributions and third-party materials.

Benchmark findings support specific observed capabilities; they do not prove the
entire theoretical Central Adaptive Cortex architecture. A future release decision
must preserve that scientific distinction and any permissions already granted.
