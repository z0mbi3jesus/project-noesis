# Participation and permissions

Community involvement is intended to help NOESIS research and development.
Useful contributions include documentation corrections, experiment proposals,
methodological criticism, and carefully distinguished observations and conclusions.

Testing becomes available only through separately approved application distribution.
Tester downloads remain on hold. No access to unpublished implementation is granted
by this wiki or by participation in issues.

## Rights

The rights notice identifies 6th Level LLC for original project materials in which
it holds rights. Contributors retain their respective rights unless separately
agreed. Participation does not transfer ownership or automatically grant a general
software-use, modification, redistribution, commercialization, or branding license.

An open-source release may be considered after a more defined and experimentally
validated foundation. It is not promised or automatic and requires explicit approval
of the release scope, rights, and license. No general reuse license is currently
selected. Applicable law, GitHub's terms, and separately granted permissions remain
unaffected.
