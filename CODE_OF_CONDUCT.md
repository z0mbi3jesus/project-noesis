# Community conduct

Effective: October 15, 2026

This policy covers project-managed issues, pull requests, research discussions,
and other community spaces when identified as official NOESIS spaces.

## Expectations

- Treat contributors respectfully, including people new to programming or research.
- Critique methods, evidence, and claims rather than attacking people.
- Distinguish observations, hypotheses, and demonstrated capabilities.
- Welcome unsuccessful experiments and constructive disagreement.
- Respect other people's privacy and attribution.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, doxxing, impersonation, spam, and
deliberate disclosure of private materials are unacceptable.

## Reporting and moderation

For non-sensitive concerns, contact the repository maintainer through an issue.
If details are sensitive, request a private contact method without posting those
details publicly. A dedicated private conduct-reporting address is not currently
published. Do not use a public issue to identify or expose someone reporting harm.

The project lead may clarify expectations, edit or remove harmful content, close
disruptive discussions, or restrict participation as appropriate. Moderation should
be proportionate and explain its basis when doing so does not expose private data.
Someone affected by a moderation decision may request reconsideration from the
project lead. No response-time guarantee is offered at this stage.
